How to Play

Armies is a single-player, turn-based game of resource engine building. Over 15 turns you build up your realm: farms and mines feed your economy, your economy pays for an army, and your army must repel three enemy invasions. When the campaign ends, your score decides how history remembers you.

Your realm

Your realm has three rows, each holding up to 5 units or buildings:

RowIts actionBase yield
🌾 FarmlandsHarvest+1 Food
⛏️ MinesMine+1 Ore
⚔️ War CampPatrol+1 Gold

This is the engine: everything you build joins one row, and most things add a bonus every time that row's action is taken. A full Farmlands row can harvest 6+ Food in one action. The more you build, the stronger every later turn becomes.

You start with 2 Food 🌾 and 1 Ore ⛏️.

Taking a turn

Each turn, do exactly one of these four things:

ActionWhat happens
🏗️ BuildPick anything from the build menu, pay its cost, and place it in its row. Its power joins your army immediately; its bonus strengthens that row's action for the rest of the game.
🌾 HarvestGain 1 Food, plus every Farmlands bonus.
⛏️ MineGain 1 Ore, plus every Mines bonus.
⚔️ PatrolGain 1 Gold, plus every War Camp bonus.

Supplies are limited, Catan-style: each unit type has a fixed stock (only one Warlord exists!). The build menu shows how many of each remain.

The three invasions ⚔️

Enemy armies attack at the end of turns 5, 10 and 15. Their strength is rolled at the start of the campaign and your scouts report it in advance — so you always know exactly what's coming, and when.

InvasionEnemy strengthRepelled: gloryDefeated: penalty
End of turn 53–5+5 🏅Raiders pillage half of every resource you hold (rounded up)
End of turn 109–13+7 🏅
End of turn 1516–22+10 🏅

You repel an invasion if your total army power is equal to or greater than the enemy's strength. Losing never ends the campaign — but pillaged stores can wreck your plans.

Strategy tips

🌱 Engine first, army second. Cheap production (Peasant Levy, Prospector, Grain Farm) built early pays for itself many times over. Raw power built late still counts fully at scoring.
⚔️ But respect the deadlines. The scouts tell you the exact enemy strength — count backwards from each invasion and make sure the power gap is closed in time. A last-minute Footman has saved many a realm.
🪙 Plan your Gold. Patrol is slow — a Gold Mine, Ox Wagon or Quartermaster working several turns in advance is what pays for a Knight or the Warlord.
🔥 Losing is (sometimes) an option. If an invasion is unwinnable, spend your stores before it hits — raiders can't pillage what you've already turned into buildings.
