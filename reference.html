Kartenburg — Reference Sheet

Four armies converge on Kartenburg, the castle of cards.

Needs: one 52-card deck · pen & paper for the glory tally · a sketched board (below) or just lay cards in rows. 1–4 players; unplayed armies run the automated script. Turn order ♥ → ♠ → ♦ → ♣ (2 players sit at ♥ and ♦).

Setup: deal each human 4 cards plus a seat bonus (♥ +0, ♠ +2, ♦ +3, ♣ +3); automated armies instead start with banked supply (♥ 0, ♠ 2, ♦ 3, ♣ 4). Flip 2 cards onto Kartenburg as the mercenary garrison (strength = their sum).

Armies

An army = a stack of up to 3 cards of your suit on one space. Strength = sum of its cards (+2 with your Queen). One army per road space; camp holds any number.

2–10Soldier (pips)AChampion (11)
JRaider (11): strikes the weakest card of any enemy road army, or infiltrates a camp (at 11 +2, + your Q) to strike an enemy Q or K post (they defend at 12 / 13); the Jack is discarded after — or deploys as a soldier Q / KCamp posts. Q: all your armies +2. K: marches cost 1 less supply

Other suits = supply: a march costs 1 supply per card in the stack (K: −1, min 1). Trade (action): swap 2 supply for 1 fresh card.

Player turn

  1. Tribute: +1 glory if you hold Kartenburg.
  2. Draw 2 (hand limit 7; discard down).
  3. Up to 2 actions: Deploy (new army, or reinforce a camp army, max 3 — marching armies and the garrison can't be reinforced) · March one space (pay supply; onto your own army = merge if ≤3; from the gate = assault) · Raid with your Jack · Trade (2 supply → 1 card).

Battle

Attacker stack total vs. defender stack total, each +2 with their Queen. Defender wins ties. Loser destroyed; winner discards its weakest card (single-card armies spared) — garrisons too. Assault winner becomes the garrison.

Reserves: a human defender may commit one card of their suit from hand before any battle resolves — its strength joins the defense, then the card is discarded (win or lose).

Siege glory: if a repelled assault still costs the garrison a casualty, the attacker takes the +1 (battering the walls); only a clean repel pays the defender.

Automated army turn — flip 2, resolve in order

Own suitQ/K to posts. Jack: raid the weakest card of the enemy army nearest Kartenburg (tie: strongest, then clockwise); nothing to raid → reinforce. Else reinforce its fullest camp army with room (oldest on ties) or found a new army.
Other suitAdd to its supply pile. If the pile ≥ the frontmost army's march cost, that army marches (gate → assault) and the pile is spent; otherwise it banks.

Glory (tally on paper)

Capture Kartenburg+5 Hold it at your turn's start+1 Hold it when the season turns+2
Win a raid / defense+1 Clean-repel an assault+1 Batter the walls (repelled but garrison bleeds)+1 to attacker

Seasons: when the deck empties, finish the round (every seat gets equal turns) — the holder of Kartenburg takes +2, discards reshuffle, season 2 begins. At the end of season 2 the war ends: most glory wins.
