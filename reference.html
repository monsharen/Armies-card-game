Four Banners — Reference Sheet

Leipzig, October 1813: four armies converge on one city — the Battle of the Nations.

Needs: one 52-card deck · pen & paper for the glory tally · a sketched board (below) or just lay cards in rows. 1–4 players; unplayed armies run the automated script. Turn order ♥ → ♠ → ♦ → ♣ (2 players sit at ♥ and ♦).

Board (sketch this)

                 [♠ camp]
                 [ 1 ]
                 [ 2 ]
                 [ 3 ]  <- gate
[♥camp][1][2][3] LEIPZIG [3][2][1][♦camp]
                 [ 3 ]
                 [ 2 ]
                 [ 1 ]
                 [♣ camp]

Setup: deal 4 to each human; flip 2 cards onto Leipzig as the mercenary garrison (strength = their sum).

Armies

An army = a stack of up to 3 cards of your suit on one space. Strength = sum of its cards (+2 with your Queen). One army per road space; camp holds any number.

2–10Soldier (pips)AChampion (11)
JRaider (11): strikes the weakest card of any enemy road army, then discard — or deploys as a soldier Q / KCamp posts. Q: all your armies +2. K: marches cost 1 less supply

Other suits = supply: a march costs 1 supply per card in the stack (K: −1, min 1).

Player turn

  1. Tribute: +1 glory if you hold Leipzig.
  2. Draw 2 (hand limit 7; discard down).
  3. Up to 2 actions: Deploy (new army, or reinforce any of your armies incl. the garrison, max 3) · March one space (pay supply; onto your own army = merge if ≤3; from the gate = assault) · Raid with your Jack.

Battle

Attacker stack total vs. defender stack total, each +2 with their Queen. Defender wins ties. Loser destroyed; winner discards its weakest card (single-card armies spared) — garrisons too. Assault winner becomes the garrison.

Automated army turn — flip 2, resolve in order

Own suitQ/K to posts. Jack: raid the weakest card of the enemy army nearest Leipzig (tie: strongest, then clockwise); nothing to raid → reinforce. Else reinforce the most forward army with room (garrison → road → camp) or found a new army.
Other suitAdd to its supply pile. If the pile ≥ the frontmost army's march cost, that army marches (gate → assault) and the pile is spent; otherwise it banks.

Glory (tally on paper)

Capture Leipzig+5 Hold it at your turn's start+1 Win a raid / repel an assault+1

Seasons: deck empty → reshuffle discards, play season 2. Deck empty again → war ends, most glory wins.
