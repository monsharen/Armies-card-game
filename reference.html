Four Banners — Reference Sheet

Needs: one 52-card deck · pen & paper for the glory tally · a sketched board (below) or just lay cards in rows. 1–4 players; unplayed armies run the automated script. Turn order ♥ → ♠ → ♦ → ♣ (2 players sit at ♥ and ♦).

Board (sketch this)

                 [♠ camp]
                 [ 1 ]
                 [ 2 ]
                 [ 3 ]  <- gate
[♥camp][1][2][3] CITADEL [3][2][1][♦camp]
                 [ 3 ]
                 [ 2 ]
                 [ 1 ]
                 [♣ camp]

One card per road space; camps unlimited. Setup: deal 4 to each human; flip 1 card onto the Citadel as the neutral garrison.

Your suit

2–10Soldier (strength = pips)AChampion (11)
JRaider (11): one-shot attack on any enemy road unit, then discard — or deploy as a soldier Q / KCamp posts. Q: all your units +2 in battle. K: marches move up to 2 spaces

Other suits = supply: discard one to pay for each march.

Player turn

  1. Draw 2 (hand limit 7; discard down).
  2. Up to 2 actions: Deploy a card of your suit · March (discard supply; 1 space, 2 with King; gate → assault Citadel; no jumping) · Raid with your Jack.

Battle

Attacker vs. defender strength, each +2 with their Queen. Defender wins ties. Loser discarded. Assault winner becomes the garrison.

Automated army turn — flip 2, resolve in order

Own suitDeploy to camp (Q/K to posts). Jack: raid the enemy unit nearest the Citadel (tie: strongest, then clockwise); no target → camp.
Other suitMarch: frontmost unit advances as far as it can (gate → assault). No road unit can move → strongest camp unit steps out. No units → wasted.

Glory (tally on paper)

Capture the Citadel+5 Hold it at season's end+3 Win a raid / defense+1

Seasons: deck empty → garrison holder +3, reshuffle discards, play season 2. Deck empty again → war ends, most glory wins.
