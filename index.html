Simple resources

Just three resources — Food, Ore and Gold. Learn it in one game, with enough depth to keep every campaign different.

⚙️

Engine building

Everything you build joins one of three rows. The more a row holds, the more its action produces — your realm snowballs turn after turn.

⚔️

Three invasions

Enemy armies strike after turns 5, 10 and 15. Your scouts report their strength in advance — build enough power in time, or watch raiders pillage your stores.

🏅

Chase your best

Single-player and turn-based: 15 turns, one action per turn, about ten minutes per campaign. Beat your personal best and climb the ranks — from Camp Follower to Legendary Conqueror.

Armies — a browser game of resource engine building. No installs, no accounts, just play.
