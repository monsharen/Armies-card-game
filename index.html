🃏

Just a deck of cards

Each suit is an army: number cards are soldiers, Aces are champions, Jacks raid, Queens are banners, Kings are generals. No components to buy — the whole game is one standard 52-card deck.

🏰

Race for the Citadel

Four roads meet at a central citadel held by mercenaries. March your column down your road, storm the gate, and hold the throne through two seasons of war.

🎲

Always four armies

Play with 1–4 players — any army without a player runs on a deterministic flip-two-cards script. Two humans? The other two armies march themselves.

✏️

Pen-and-paper ready

Everything here works on a kitchen table: the board is nine boxes and a cross you can sketch in a minute, the cards are the units, and the only bookkeeping is a glory tally. Print the reference sheet and go.
